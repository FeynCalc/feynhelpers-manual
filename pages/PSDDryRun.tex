\section{PSDDryRun}\label{psddryrun}\index{PSDDryRun}}

\texttt{PSDDryRun} is an option for \texttt{PSDCreatePythonScripts} and
other functions of the pySecDec interface.

When set to \texttt{True}, no files will be written to the disk.
